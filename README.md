# Write-Off Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026
Write-Off ("the app") is a receipt, expense and invoice app made by Masee. This policy explains what happens to your information.

## Your receipts and records stay on your phone

Receipts, photos, amounts, clients, invoices and settings are stored only on your device. Receipts are read (OCR) on the device. Nothing you enter is uploaded to us, and we have no servers that store your records.

## Optional location stamp

If you turn on "Add location stamp" in Settings, the app saves your approximate location with each new receipt, on your device only. It is off by default, and you can turn it off at any time.

## Purchases

If you buy a subscription or a one-time purchase, Google Play processes the payment. We use RevenueCat to check which plan you have. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history, but not your receipts or records. See RevenueCat's privacy policy (https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) and Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Sharing

Information leaves your device only when you choose to share it, for example by sending an invoice, a PDF, a CSV export or a backup through your phone's share menu. The app you share to has its own privacy policy.

## Notifications

Return and warranty reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

## Ads and analytics

The app contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## Deleting your data

Delete receipts inside the app, use Settings → Delete all data, or uninstall the app. To delete purchase records held by RevenueCat, contact us.

## Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
